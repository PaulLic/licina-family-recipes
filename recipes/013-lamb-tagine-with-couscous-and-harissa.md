## Ingredients

Marinade:
- 60 ml (¼ cup) olive oil
- a pinch of saffron threads
- 1 tsp ground ginger
- 1 tsp ground paprika
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- grated zest of 1 lemon
- a pinch of freshly ground black pepper

Lamb tagine:
- 800 g lamb leg or shoulder, diced
- olive oil
- 3 onions, sliced
- 4 garlic cloves, sliced
- 600 ml tomato juice
- 600 ml white chicken stock
- 1 cinnamon stick, broken
- 150 g (1 cup) whole blanched almonds
- sea salt and freshly ground black pepper
- 4 tomatoes, blanched, peeled, seeded and chopped
- 60 g (½ cup) raisins
- 2 tbsp honey
- 12 small new potatoes, cut in half
- 1 large handful of coriander leaves, roughly chopped
- ½ quantity harissa

Couscous:
- 370 g (2 cups) couscous
- 500 ml white chicken stock or water, boiling
- a knob of unsalted butter
- juice of ½ lemon
- ½ salted lemon, flesh discarded and zest finely diced
- 1 handful of flat-leaf parsley leaves, chopped

## Method

1. Combine all of the marinade ingredients and mix well. Put the lamb in a non-reactive shallow tray and pour over the marinade to coat. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
2. Preheat the oven to 180°C.
3. Heat a little olive oil in a heavy-based frying pan over high heat and seal the lamb in batches, then set aside. Reduce the heat to medium, add the onion and garlic to the pan and cook for several minutes, until the onion is translucent. Add the tomato juice and chicken stock, stirring to loosen any sediment. Return the lamb to the pan with the cinnamon stick and almonds, and season with sea salt and black pepper. Bring to the boil, then remove from the heat. Transfer to a tagine pot or earthenware dish and cook in the oven for 1½ hours, or until the lamb is tender.
4. Remove the lamb from the tagine with a slotted spoon and keep warm. Add the tomato, raisins, honey and potato to the tagine and cook for 15–20 minutes, until the potato is tender and the sauce has thickened. Return the lamb to the tagine to heat through.
5. Meanwhile, make the couscous: put the couscous in a large bowl, pour over the boiling stock or water, stir briefly, then cover and set aside to steam for 10–15 minutes. Fluff with a fork, then stir through the butter, lemon juice, salted lemon and parsley, and season with sea salt and black pepper.
6. To serve, spoon the tagine into serving dishes with the couscous and scatter over the coriander. Serve the harissa separately on the side.

## Chef's Tips

1. A tagine is a Moroccan terracotta cooking dish with a conical lid that traps the aromas of the food as it slowly cooks — a good cast-iron pot works just as well if you don't have one.
