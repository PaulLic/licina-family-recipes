## Ingredients

**Dough**

- 20 g fresh yeast
- 2–3 tbsp lukewarm milk
- A little flour and a pinch of salt, for the starter
- 750 g plain flour (the original calls for *oštro brašno*, a coarse-ground flour)
- 250 g butter
- 2 large tbsp icing sugar
- 4 egg yolks, plus 1 more for glazing
- A little lemon zest

**Walnut filling**

- ¼ cup milk
- 1 vanilla bean
- 200 g sugar
- 250 g walnuts, ground
- Zest of ½ lemon
- 100 g sultanas
- Vanilla sugar, for dusting

## Method

Dissolve the yeast in the lukewarm milk, stir through a little flour and a pinch of salt, and leave it in a warm place to rise. If the yeast is fresh it will be ready in about 10 minutes, and you can go straight on to the dough.

Sift the flour onto a kneading board and add the risen yeast, butter, icing sugar, egg yolks and lemon zest. Bring it together into a dough and leave it to rest for half an hour in the same spot — it needs neither warmth nor cold. Then roll it out to ½ cm thick and cut out rounds with a cutter.

For the filling, bring the milk to the boil in a saucepan, then add the vanilla bean, sugar and ground walnuts. Boil it well, take it off the heat, grate in the zest of half a lemon and stir through the sultanas. Mix well and leave to cool.

Place a teaspoon of the walnut filling in the centre of each round, roll it up into a crescent and curve it neatly. Brush each one with egg yolk and set them on a buttered tray. Bake in a moderate oven (around 180°C), and dust with vanilla sugar while still warm.
