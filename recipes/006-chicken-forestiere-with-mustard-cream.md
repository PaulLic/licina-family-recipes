## Ingredients

- 4 chicken breasts (skin-on preferred, skinless OK)
- 2 rashers bacon, chopped
- 100g small mushrooms, sliced
- 2 cloves garlic, finely chopped
- 2 stalks green shallots, chopped
- 30g butter
- 1 cup white wine
- 300ml whipping cream
- 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
